# Moon Lovers: Scarlet Heart Ryeo

**Genre:** Historical, Romance, Fantasy, Melodrama  
**Episodes:** 20  
**Release Year:** 2016  

## Synopsis
During a total solar eclipse, a 21st-century woman named *Go Ha-jin* is transported back in time to the **Goryeo Dynasty**. She wakes up in the year 941 inside the body of **Hae Soo** and encounters the many royal princes of the ruling Wang family.

## Main Characters
* **Hae Soo / Go Ha-jin** (played by *IU*) - A modern woman trapped in the Goryeo era.
* **Wang So** (played by *Lee Joon-gi*) - The feared and misunderstood 4th Prince.
* **Wang Wook** (played by *Kang Ha-neul*) - The gentle and intelligent 8th Prince.
* **Wang Jung** (played by *Ji Soo*) - The loyal 14th Prince.

## Top 3 Reasons to Watch
1. **Unforgettable Chemistry:** The emotional journey between Hae Soo and Wang So.
2. **Iconic OST:** Incredible soundtrack featuring songs like *For You*, *Say Yes*, and *My Love*.
3. **Visuals & Cinematography:** Beautiful traditional Korean outfits and palace settings.

## Memorable Quote
> "If we are not from the same world, then I will find you, my Soo." — **Wang So**
